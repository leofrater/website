The Full Diary System

01 Strategy & Targeting

We start with the job you actually want more of — not “kitchens” but the full refit, in the postcodes worth the drive. The offer, the angle and the audience are built around that one buyer before a penny reaches Meta.

02 Scripts & On-Site Filming

Every script written here, then a full content day in your showroom or on a live install. Ten hooks filmed against interchangeable bodies, so when one tires we swap it instead of starting over. Your projects, your finishes, your team. Never stock footage.

03 The Tyre-Kicker Quiz

Every click runs into a short qualifying quiz before it ever counts as a lead. Location, budget, timeline, cash or finance. Browsers and bargain-hunters fall out here, quietly, before they reach a human being.

04 Straight Into The CRM

What survives lands in GoHighLevel the second it is submitted, tagged and timestamped with every quiz answer attached. Nothing sits in an inbox waiting to be noticed.

05 I Call Every One

Not a text. A phone call, from me, the same working day — confirming the budget is real, the timeline is real, and that both decision-makers will actually be in the room.

06 Booked Into Your Diary

Only then does it reach you. The appointment lands in your CRM with everything I learned on the call attached. You turn up and sell.
