Privacy notice

This explains how we use business contact details when we reach out to companies, and how to stop hearing from us.

Who we are

Foxd Media is a trading name of FOXED MEDIA LTD, registered in England and Wales (company number 17455882), registered office 68 Trinity Street, Shrewsbury, SY3 7PD. We are the data controller for the information described here.

Contact: leo@foxdmedia.co.uk
ICO registration number: registration in progress

Why you've heard from us

We contact businesses that may benefit from our advertising services (Meta and Google ads for UK service businesses). We only email limited companies and similar organisations, at their business email address.

What information we hold

Business contact details: your name, job title or role, business email address, business phone number, company name, company number, business address and town, website, the type of work the business does, and a record of our emails to you and any replies.

Where we got it

From public and business sources: the Companies House register, your business website and online business listings (such as Google Maps), and business contact databases. Each email tells you which source we used for you.

Why we use it, and our lawful basis

To offer our services to your business and follow up on any reply. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f)): contacting businesses about services relevant to them, in a limited way, with an easy way to opt out. We have weighed this against your interests and keep our contact short and infrequent.

Your right to object, and how to stop emails

Reply "no" (or "unsubscribe") to any email and we will stop straight away and add your address to a do-not-contact list so you are not contacted again. You can also email us at the contact address above.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers who help us run our email outreach, acting on our instructions: our email and inbox providers (Google Workspace, supplied through InboxKit), our email sending platform (PlusVibe), and email-address verification services (such as MillionVerifier). We never sell your information.

Some of these providers are based outside the UK. Where they are, your information is protected by UK adequacy regulations or the UK's approved data transfer contracts.

How long we keep it

Up to 24 months from our last contact with you, then we delete it. If you opt out, we keep only your email address on our do-not-contact list, for as long as we do outreach, so that we never contact you again.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your information, ask us to correct or delete it, ask us to restrict how we use it, or object to our use of it. Contact us at the address above; we'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113).
